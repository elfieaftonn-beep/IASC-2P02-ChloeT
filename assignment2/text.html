a silent voice follows Shoya Ishida, a high school student currently contemplating suicide. He quits his job, sells all his things and prepares to jump off a bridge. He flashes back to his elementary school days when he still had friends, and his teacher introduced a new student named Shoko Nishymia. At first glance, something is different about Shoko, with her communicating to the class via noteBook. Shoko then tells the class what we all suspected. Shoko is deaf. Frustrated by his inability to understand her, Shoya begins to bully and harass Shoko. Even making fun of her disabilities and steal her hearing aids. Shoko tries to befriend her other classmates but Uno Naioko and the other girls just get fed up with her disability, and isolate her. Shoya’s friends do not intervene with his bullying, despite being key witnesses. One day, Shoya tears out Shoko’s hearing aids (quite literally) causing her ear to bleed and scarring her. After this incident the principal asks of Shoko is being bullied, and the teacher demands that Shoya stand up. He tries to plead his case and say that he wasn’t the only one, and that nobody did anything to stop him, but he is instead used as a scapegoat by his classmates, and they all deny it. Shoya is taken to mrs. Nishymia to apologise, and only feels guilty when he sees the large amount of money that his mother is forced to pay for Shoko’s hearing aids. Shoko transfers school and Shoya’s friends outcast him, treating him the same way, if not more brutally than, he did to Shoko, isolating him from others. years later in high school Shoya has completely isolated himself from everyone else. He refuses to make eye contact with others (the most basic form of intimacy and respect) and walks around school looking at the ground with his hands over his ears to drown out the noise. Eventually Ishida decides that if this is how things are, then he’ll just die sooner than later, taking us back to the beginning. Though, Ishida wants to cut all loose ends, for example, paying back his mother. The one thing he wants to do the most is give Shoko her notebook back. The very same notebook that she used to comunicate to the Class.
